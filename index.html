How YES/NO Arbitrage Works

Polymarket markets are binary YES/NO. In a fair, efficient market P(YES) + P(NO) = 1.00. When the order book is thin or slow to update, a gap appears — this is what automated Polymarket CLOB arbitrage targets: 

P(YES) = 0.48
P(NO)  = 0.48
──────────────────
Sum    = 0.96  ← 4% edge. Bot detects this, sizes bets, fires orders on both sides.

FAQ

What is a Polymarket arbitrage bot?

A Polymarket arbitrage bot is automated software that monitors prediction market prices and executes trades when mispriced opportunities appear. This open-source bot connects to the Polymarket CLOB API, detects edges in real time, and places orders without manual input.

How does YES/NO arbitrage work on Polymarket?

In a binary market, fair pricing means YES + NO = $1.00. When the sum drops below $1.00, buying both sides locks in a guaranteed profit at resolution. This intra-market arbitrage bot automates that detection and execution.

What arbitrage strategies does this Polymarket bot support?

Intra-market arbitrage, multi-outcome bundle arbitrage, latency/temporal arbitrage, correlation/logical arbitrage, tail-end resolution arbitrage, and partial cross-platform arbitrage. Market making is on the roadmap.

Is this Polymarket trading bot open source?

Yes — MIT licensed, written in TypeScript, available on GitHub. Includes dry-run, scan-only, and live trading modes so developers can study or deploy automated Polymarket arbitrage safely.

About

polymarket-arbitrage-bot is an open-source Polymarket trading bot and Polymarket arbitrage bot built in TypeScript. It connects to the Polymarket CLOB API to read order books, monitor market prices in real time, and execute automated trades on prediction markets running on the Polygon network. The arbitrage module detects when outcome probabilities on a Polymarket market are mispriced — meaning the sum of YES and NO prices is below 1.00 — and exploits that edge by placing simultaneous orders on both sides. The trading module supports limit and market orders, configurable position sizing (Kelly criterion or flat bet), and built-in risk controls including max exposure limits and daily drawdown stops. This polymarket bot is aimed at developers who want a fully automated, open-source starting point for Polymarket automation, prediction market trading, or studying how CLOB-based arbitrage works in practice.
